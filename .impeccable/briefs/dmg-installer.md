<!-- impeccable:surface-brief-schema 1 -->

## Mode

Persuade

## Purpose

First open of the MacCleaner disk image: one clear install action — drag the app onto Applications.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm blue-glass drop target, not a blank Finder tray — brand and instruction before chrome.
OWN-WORLD: Night indigo canvas, frosted circular pads, soft cyan drag arrow, copper underline under MacCleaner wordmark; matches DESIGN.md tokens.
STORY: Visitor sees MacCleaner, understands drag-to-Applications, completes install without reading a readme.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark + “Drag to Applications” top-center; left pad for MacCleaner.app; right pad for Applications; cyan arrow between; privacy line at foot.
FORM: Extension of established blue-glass Mac utility world; no new identity.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
